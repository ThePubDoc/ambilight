Streaming video

Streaming services black out their video while the screen is captured. While one of these is the window in front (in any browser or app), the screen modes switch to another mode. Other sites, like YouTube, keep screen sync.

App profiles

Use a different mode while a particular app is in front, e.g. Mix for games or Sound for a music player. Everything else uses the mode chosen in the panel.
